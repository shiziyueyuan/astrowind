## Decide how someone might enter

This website began as a place for the work and ideas of 时咨月圆 / Shea. I wanted a little of the freedom of exploring a game: visitors could choose an entrance, open a few windows and follow their curiosity.

The homepage gradually became an experimental desktop. ENTRY, IDENTITY, ARCHIVE, LAB and SIGNAL lead to a welcome, an introduction, projects, experiments and contact details. Windows can move freely and stay open together; projects and writing have their own reading pages.

## Turn feedback into the next visible change

Vibe Coding here meant making decisions throughout an existing template. The official open source AstroWind template provided the foundation. AI helped implement components, styles and configuration, while my feedback set the direction: freely draggable windows, an opening animation and a first screen that felt more like a computer desktop.

Further revisions changed the fonts, the SHEA wordmark and the icon colours. Chinese, English and Japanese use suitable free, open source fonts. White and pale green define the light theme; black and orange define the dark one. The theme follows the system by default and can be changed manually. Each request became a concrete revision, followed by another look at the page.

## Make room for work and unfinished thoughts

The archive started with a privacy-conscious account of the stone arch profile study, followed by the collaborative puzzle video. These entries retain the making process and acknowledge shared contributions without presenting team work as a solo achievement.

LAB holds Vibe Coding, Vibe Videoing and everyday writing. The local writing studio imports Markdown, keeps private drafts and lets a reviewed local version be synced to the public site. Public pages are for reading, with no comments or replies. Contact channels also changed in response to use: equally styled account-copy actions replaced the email link that was not working reliably.

## From a local preview to a shareable address

Going online made the next set of details visible. A working local page still needed its published links, fonts and media checked. GitHub Pages uses a repository subpath, so language routes, scripts and video URLs had to account for it. Video uploads also hit network timeouts; incremental transfers eventually completed the upload while preserving the existing website edition's quality and content.

The first release went to GitHub Pages, with the online build, language pages, fonts and video resources checked. The desktop is now available through its [public entrance](https://shiziyueyuan.github.io/astrowind/).

## Put this version out into the world

This is still an evolving demo. Projects, writing and interaction details will continue to grow. These notes keep a record of its current form and the revisions between a request and a website someone can actually open and explore.
